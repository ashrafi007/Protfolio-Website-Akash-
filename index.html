About

IntazarAkash

S M Intazar Ahmed Akash is a Dhaka-based model working across fashion, commercial, TVC, OVC and runway.

At 6'0" with a lean frame and signature dark curls, he moves easily between sharp tailoring, contemporary streetwear and traditional menswear, a range that suits both editorial and mainstream commercial briefs.

Akash has served as the brand face of Hazzel Fashion. Comfortable in stills and on camera, he brings the same composure to a tight editorial frame as to a fast-moving TVC set.

He is available for bookings in Dhaka and across Bangladesh.
