## 3. Visual strategy
Minimalist high-fashion, gallery-white; photos carry the colour. Off-white #F7F5F2, ink near-black, no accent. Bodoni Moda + Inter. Max two images per row desktop, one mobile. No filters, shadows or auto sliders. Never crop feet. WebP ~300 KB, lazy-loaded.

- Home: split hero (suit shot + stacked name, categories, height, CTAs) → three category tiles → Hazzel Fashion band → closing CTA.
- Portfolio: tabs, alternating full-width and pairs, lightbox with credits, 8–15 images per tab.
- Motion: muted showreel with play button, clip grid tagged TVC / OVC / Runway with client and year.
- Digitals: plain grid — headshot front/three-quarter, both profiles, full length front/side/back, smiling/neutral, hair loose and tied. "Updated: Month YYYY". Refresh every 3–6 months.
- About: portrait + bio + stats + experience; hair range strip below.
- Book: quiet, form left, contacts and comp card right.

## 5. Booking copy
Heading: Bookings & Castings. Include: client/agency, project type, dates + call times + fittings, location, usage (media, territory, duration), rate or budget, grooming/wardrobe, option or confirmed.

Good to know: replies within 24 hours Sunday–Thursday; options held 48 hours; self-tapes in 24–48 hours; hair can be loose, tied or under headwear — cuts or colour changes agreed in advance.
